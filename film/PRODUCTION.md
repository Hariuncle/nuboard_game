# THE LAST GARDENER — production bible

## Deliverable

- Independent science-fiction short feature, unrelated to the game.
- Minimum final runtime: 15:00.
- Production slate: 60 generated shots, each 15 seconds (H3 frame grid: 362 frames at 24 fps).
- Master: 1920×1080, 24 fps, H.264, yuv420p, AAC stereo.
- H3 base generation: 1344×768, 24 fps, native 32 kHz stereo audio.
- No still-image zooms, duplicated loops, watermarks, or fake motion.

## Story

In 2197, Earth has become a drowned archive and humanity's last viable seeds orbit above it in a failing greenhouse. Dr. Hana Seo, the station's elderly final gardener, discovers that one seed is responding to a dormant planetary root network. With Ilyeon, a young maintenance android gradually reclaimed by moss, she descends to the flooded city, crosses its memory-haunted ruins, and reaches the cathedral-sized remains of the world's last tree. Restarting the network will save the biosphere but consume the power keeping Ilyeon alive. Hana plants the seed; Ilyon chooses to become the bridge. Years later, the city is a wetland forest and a repaired, living Ilyeon watches children release paper birds beside Hana.

## License gate

MiniMax H3's public Community License lists the Republic of Korea as an Excluded Territory. The local open-weight workflow must therefore run only when KT Cloud or the operator can document a separate applicable licence/permission. The presence of model files in a former session is not by itself proof of that permission. Until this gate is documented, keep the workflow and assets ready but do not download or redeploy H3 weights into a new Korean-region session.

## Acceptance gates

1. Every shot validates against the live ComfyUI `object_info` before queueing.
2. Every finished shot contains H.264 video and AAC stereo audio, 1344×768, 24 fps.
3. Duration per generated shot is at least 14.8 seconds.
4. Frozen-video detection rejects shots with insufficient frame difference.
5. Audio-silence detection rejects shots whose integrated loudness is effectively absent.
6. Final runtime is at least 900 seconds and final frame size is 1920×1080.
7. Title/credits may be added during mastering but cannot be used to pad missing narrative runtime.
